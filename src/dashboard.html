Weather stations: readings from Met Office climate stations, so each figure is for that exact spot. Some stations have no sunshine recorder. Council areas: the Met Office HadUK-Grid 1991-2020 average over the area's 5 km squares, weighted by how much of each square lies inside it, then shifted to the period picked above using how much the nearest few stations' readings differ from their own 1991-2020 grid values. Council areas larger than about 1,000 km² (52 of them, such as North Yorkshire and Highland) are split into pieces of about that size and named after their biggest town or village. Pieces in hilly country still average mountains, glens and coast together, so the figure may not match where people actually live. Days with rain cover 1991-2020 for every place. Length of day is calculated from latitude.
